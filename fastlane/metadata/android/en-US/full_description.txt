ComicReader is a native comic reader for Android phones and tablets.

Read CBZ, ZIP, CBR, RAR and PDF files, import local comics, or connect MEGA and HTTPS WebDAV. Download comics for offline reading and keep progress, bookmarks and favorites.

Choose a cover grid or list, filter read and unread comics, and pull down to refresh folders. The reader includes zoom, page fit, manga direction, brightness and optional volume-key navigation. Turn forward after the final page to continue with the next clear unread number in the same series and folder.

Three resizable home-screen widgets show current reads and upcoming issues. Light, dark and OLED themes, accent colors, English and Spanish are included.

This F-Droid build contains no Google Play Services. Google Drive sign-in and synchronization are unavailable in this build. MEGA, WebDAV and local reading remain available. Updates are managed by F-Droid rather than the in-app GitHub APK installer.

Background covers and downloads respect separate mobile-data preferences. Generating a missing cloud cover may require a temporary comic download and uses provider transfer quota. Comics download before reading; encrypted and multi-volume RAR archives are unsupported.

Donations through Ko-fi are optional and do not unlock features. The support link opens your browser only when you choose it.
