Fixes displaced and undersized button icons and inconsistent control alignment.

- Icon controls use centered native image buttons. Glyphs scale to the available area in toolbar and circular controls without inherited text metrics or background tint.
- Outline icons use a consistent rounded stroke; the library uses an open book, a clock without the history arrow, filled provider symbols and a dedicated zoom icon. Licensed Lucide and Material assets are bundled with their notices.
- Reader tools and previous/next arrows share an icon baseline. Labels and source cards adapt to large fonts; chips retain compact visible shapes and 48dp touch areas.
- Comic overflow controls align with their titles and retain expanded 48dp touch targets. Settings use consistently sized switches and aligned row icons.

Validation: 42 passing JVM tests, including native Android framework pixel/layout tests; 24 locally rendered library/reader/settings screens across eight configurations; debug, Android-test and minified release builds; lint and formatting. The regression tests fail against the previous centering/size implementation. Physical OnePlus/OEM rendering and the instrumented Android suite remain unverified.
