Adds **CBR/RAR** support to MEGA and the reader. The previous file filter accepted only CBZ/ZIP and PDF, hiding CBR files.

- CBR and RAR appear in MEGA, Drive, WebDAV and local imports.
- Read RAR4 and RAR5, including compressed/solid archives, with natural page ordering and navigation.
- Covers, offline reading, favorites and progress work with the new formats.
- Junrar 8.1.2 is pinned, with no additional JNI libraries and explicit decompression/dictionary limits.

After installation, refresh the library in settings to retrieve files omitted by the older catalog.

Archives must be unencrypted and single-volume. Limits: 64 MiB per entry/dictionary, 4 GiB total unpacked size and 10,000 pages. Solid archives can take longer to seek backwards. 7z remains unsupported.

Install over the previous version to keep your library, accounts and progress. Use `ComicReader-arm64-v8a.apk` for ARM64 phones and tablets. The original test signing certificate is retained. Android and Play Protect decide whether installation is allowed; protections are not disabled. Version 0.4 can discover this release through its update menu.
