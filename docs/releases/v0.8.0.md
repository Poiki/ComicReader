Adds home-screen widgets and continuation between numbered comics.

- Three resizable widgets: Continue reading, Next issue and Reading shelf. Larger layouts show more covers, titles and progress, up to nine comics. They follow the app's theme, accent and English/Spanish language, with preview images in the widget picker.
- Widgets read saved listings and cover caches, with coalesced updates after leaving the app. They have no polling job and never download comics in the background. Tap a cover to read or download it through a private app entry point.
- Turning forward from the rendered final page marks the comic complete and opens the lowest higher unread number in the same series, folder and account. Ambiguous numbering stops continuation. Offline copies open immediately; remote continuation refreshes its folder and respects account identity and the mobile-data preference.
- New local/cloud imports retain source-folder metadata. Existing MEGA links are reused; old imports whose original folders are unknown share the local library scope. Content-based progress survives removing and downloading a copy again.

Validation: 85 passing JVM tests; 49 widget renders plus the existing 26 app/provider renders; debug, Android-test and minified release builds; lint and formatting. Tests cover responsive layouts, widget clicks, bounded bitmap/IPC memory, completion/save ordering, source-folder retention, account changes, network handovers and a synthetic cloud-to-reader flow. Live accounts, physical-device and launcher behavior, battery consumption and the instrumented Android suite remain unverified.

Install over the existing version to preserve accounts, offline files and progress. The ARM64 APK is intended for ARM64 phones and tablets. The original test signing certificate is retained.
