Adds optional local-copy cleanup after finishing a comic.

- Settings → Library and downloads → Delete local copy after finishing is off by default and available in English and Spanish.
- After advancing past the final page, progress is saved before the reader closes the archive and removes its private copy. Original files, cloud files, progress, bookmarks and favorites are preserved. Finishing without a next issue closes the reader when cleanup is enabled.
- Next-issue lookup can use retained cloud progress after the finished comic's local copy has been removed. Cached listings and account boundaries remain in place.
- Reaching the last page or leaving an unfinished comic keeps its copy. The setting applies to finishing in the reader, rather than marking existing items read in the library. Cleanup errors are logged and shown as a short message.

Validation: host tests check the off-by-default setting and persistence, untouched copies with the option disabled, successful close-before-delete cleanup, retained progress/bookmarks/favorites/original bytes, preserved next and unfinished comics, failed progress commits, and cloud continuation after removing the preceding local copy. The existing layout, widget and archive checks remain in place. Physical Android storage behavior and live cloud-account transfers remain unverified.

Standard and Google-free APKs retain the original signing certificate for updates. Install over the existing app to retain local data.
